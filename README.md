# The Golden Road to Samarkand

An Oregon Trail–style game set on the Silk Road in 1404. Three roads, from Tabriz, Delhi or Constantinople, lead a caravan of travelers, camels and one goat to Timur's great feast in Samarkand.

The title comes from James Elroy Flecker's 1913 poem *The Golden Journey to Samarkand* ("We take the Golden Road to Samarkand").

Single-file HTML game: open `index.html` in a browser. Pixel art is drawn procedurally on a canvas; music and sound are synthesized with WebAudio.
